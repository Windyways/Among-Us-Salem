# Among-Us-Salem Ⅳ
An among us mod that adds +4 new roles to among us!
These roles are exactly how they would work in the Town of Salem games. You may find roles from:
Town of Salem
Better Town of Salem
Town Of Salem 2
Better Town Of Salem 2
(I will not be adding any Traitors In Salem roles, since 90% of the roles have overlapping names)

Among Us Salem Discord: https://discord.gg/vk8MKwkbdd
Among Us Salem TierListMaker: https://tiermaker.com/create/among-us-salem--17268167-2


--- TOWN ROLES ---
The Town is the equivilant to Crewmates, their goal is to eject all evildoers or by completing their tasks to banish the Necronomicon.

#Pilgrim
The Pilgrim is a Town Outlier role that has no special abilities.

#Sheriff
The Sheriff is a Town Investigative role that prioritizes searching players for incriminating evidence, being a big threat against the Mafia and the Coven.


--- MAFIA ROLES ---
The Mafia is the equivilant to Impostors, their goal is to kill all that oppose them. Only a few Mafia roles can kill, so when there are no more alive Mafia Killing roles, a member of the Mafia will be promoted to a Mafioso, making sure the Mafia team always has a way to kill.

#Mafioso
The Mafioso is a Mafia Killing role that performs kills for the Godfather, and is promoted to Godfather if one dies. Although if there is no Godfather, it can kill on its own freely.

--- COVEN ROLES ---
The Coven is a third party faction that has the same goal as the Mafia, take down everyone that opposes them. One Coven member will obtain the Necronomicon, granting them the ability to kill while the remaining Coven cannot kill during the Night unless they have a unique ability to do so.

Covenite
The Covenite is a Coven Outlier role that has no unique ability aside from being able to attack others while it has the Necronomicon.
